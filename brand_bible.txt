Kushel Wellness Club
Brand Bible
Version 1.0
This document is the single source of truth for the Kushel Wellness Club brand and should be used together with the implementation_plan.md during website development. It defines the identity, philosophy, content, structure, user journey, and functional expectations for the MVP website.
Purpose
Build a premium, community-first wellness website that helps visitors become engaged community members through education, guidance, accountability and sustainable habits.
Brand Identity
Name: Kushel Wellness Club
Tagline: Building Healthier Lives, One Habit at a Time.
Positioning: A community-driven wellness platform helping individuals and families build healthier lifestyles.
Mission
Helping individuals and families build healthier lifestyles through personalized guidance, balanced nutrition, supportive community, daily accountability, and sustainable habits.
Vision
To build India's most trusted wellness community, empowering families to become healthier together.
