Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Kushel Wellness Club.

Our coaching is highly personalized. You start with a free wellness assessment to understand your goals. Based on the assessment, Coach Anjala and the team create a tailored nutrition and lifestyle plan for you. You will receive daily accountability check-ins, access to community experiences, and ongoing support to ensure you stay on track.

Yes, the majority of our coaching and community experiences are conducted online, allowing you to participate from anywhere in India. We use WhatsApp for daily accountability and host weekly sessions like "Motivational Monday" and "Transformational Tuesday" via video calls.

No, you do not need a gym membership to see results. Our approach focuses primarily on balanced nutrition (80%) and an active lifestyle (20%). We provide recommendations for home-based workouts, walking routines, and active family activities that fit seamlessly into your day.

Results vary from person to person depending on their starting point and consistency. Many members notice improvements in energy levels and digestion within the first 1-2 weeks. Visible weight management and habit transformation usually become evident within 30 to 90 days of consistent effort.

The first step is simply to book a FREE Wellness Consultation with us. You can do this by clicking the "Contact" button or messaging us directly on WhatsApp. During the call, we will discuss your goals and determine the best program for you.
