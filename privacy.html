Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At Kushel Wellness Club, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you fill out a contact form, use our wellness calculators, or communicate with us via email or WhatsApp. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and any health-related information you choose to share.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide personalized wellness coaching, improve our services, communicate with you, and send you relevant information about our programs and community events.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@kushelwellness.com.
